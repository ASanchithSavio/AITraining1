# TV Shows prompt review gates

Use these gates with the general method. Exact series, performers, report serials, counts, URLs, and outcomes remain in ignored local notes.

## Series and episode identity

- Freeze the original network season, series, sampled installment, and report date. Do not rely on current streaming catalogues or fan-edited episode lists.
- Use performer or character evidence from institutional catalogues, archives, or interviews to select the series without naming it outright.
- Distinguish a spinoff or successor series from the earlier program in which its characters appeared.
- Record same-report rows for predecessor episodes, adjacent serials, specials, and similarly titled programs.

## Two-table crosswalks

- Prefer a terminal publication that separates a title-to-serial index from the characteristics table, so both joins are necessary.
- Verify the index page and characteristics page visually and independently; do not assume that adjacent serials retain title order across sections.
- Ask for a directly printed characteristic. Do not count events by watching an episode or calculate an average across installments.
- Keep the internal serial, report title, page numbers, table numbers, and answer out of the prompt.

## Evidence and shortcuts

- Use independent sources for the cast or character identity and the historical measured characteristic.
- Search the title with the characteristic label and proposed value. Reject any candidate whose full pair appears in an indexed snippet.
- Exclude predecessor or adjacent rows only when they are genuine in-scope confusers.
- Require human-readable HTML/PDF evidence and exact page locators; do not use JSON, YAML, fan wikis, or an unbounded text search as terminal proof.
